You're almost ready to access all the features of the Pokémon Global Link!

Check your email for a message from pokemon.com. Follow the instructions in the message to activate your account.

If you do not receive the email from pokemon.com within 48 hours, please do the following:

  1. Check your spam or junk mail folder for the email
  2. If the email is not in your spam or junk mail folder, resend the activation email now.
  3. If you still arent receiving the activation email, go to the New Account Troubleshooting page in the Customer Service section.

Add pokemon.com to your email contacts or address book to prevent future messages from going into your spam or junk mail folder.
